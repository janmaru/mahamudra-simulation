## Verification notes

Findings from the 2026-09-11 check that affected chapter text:

- **No dedicated search for cubic-lattice anisotropy in UHECR arrival
  directions has been published** by Auger, Telescope Array or their joint
  working group. The Beane–Davoudi–Savage prediction is *proposed, not
  tested*. Chapters 2.1.1, 3.2, 4.2.1 and 7 were corrected accordingly.
- **Campbell experiments:** the Kickstarter ran 14 May – 13 June 2018 (not
  2021 as an earlier draft said, following a 2021 news item); ~$237k raised;
  work assigned in 2019 to a group at Cal Poly Pomona; **no result published
  as of September 2026**.
- **Hubble tension:** not resolved by JWST; SH0ES-side consensus 73.50 ± 0.81
  (2026) vs Planck 67.4 ± 0.5, 7.1σ; CCHP gives an intermediate ~70.4.
- **Muon g-2:** with the 2025 lattice-based Standard Model value the tension
  is ≈ 0.6σ; the disagreement has become theory-vs-theory (lattice + CMD-3
  vs older e⁺e⁻ data).
- **Infodynamics:** no independent replication; one peer-reviewed critique
  (Crecraft 2025) and a self-correction by the author (Vopson 2025).
- **Bostrom's ~20 %:** primary source is the 2007 NYT interview; the FAQ
  carried the figure until the 2025 revision removed it.

Details not independently confirmable (publisher pages blocked): the
page range of the Zurek volume for [Wheeler 1990] (secondary sources
agree on 3–28); priority between the Bantam and Gollancz 1964 editions of
[Galouye 1964].
